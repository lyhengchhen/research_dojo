### NLP and LLMs
NLP is a field in machine learning focused on understanding everything related to human language. 
- Unndestand the context/relationship of the words

Tasks of NLPs: 
- **Classifying whole sentences:** getting the sentiment of a review, detecting if an email is spam, determining if a sentence is grammatically correct or whether two sentence are logically related or not. 
- **Classifying each word in sentence:** identifying the grammatical components of a sentence (noun, adverb, verb, adjective)
- **Generating text content:** complete the sentence, fill in the component of the sentence, etc
- **Extracting an answer from a text:** extracting the answer to the question based on the information provided in the context. 
- **Generating a new sentence from an input text:** translating a text into another language, summarizing,..
- **Subfield:** also help in Speech recognition and CV like generating the transcrip of audio sample, or description of the image


        LLM is an AI model trained on massive amounts of text data that can understand and generate human-like text, recognize the patterns in language, and perform a wide variety of language tasks without task-specific training. They represent a significant advancement in the field of NLP.


#### Characteristic of the LLMs
- **Scale:** contain millions, billions, or hundred of billion even trillion  of parameters
- **General capacities:** they can perform multiple tasks without task-specific training
- **In-context learning:** they can learn from examples provided in the prompt
- **Emergent abilities:** as these models grow in size, they demonstrate capabilities that were not explicitly programmed or anticipated. 


#### Problem with LLMs
- **Hallucination:** generate plausible/incorrect information confidently
- **Bias:** reproduce the biases present the training data or in the input 
- **Lack of true understanding:** does not really have the real understanding of the real world
- **Context window:** limited
- **Computational resource:** require large resources
